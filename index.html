Structural Self-Analysis

  
Where does your effort actually go?


  

    Every person operates inside institutional architectures — workplaces, schools, families, communities. Each one has a suppression gap: the distance between what you are capable of contributing and what actually reaches decisions. Name yours.
  


  


  

    
What is producing this gap?

    

      Suppression gaps are not personal failures. They are structural features. The authority-weighted filter, the evidence policy, the block fields operating in your institution — these determine what routes and what doesn’t. Name the mechanism you see most clearly in your situation.
